# People Research — Public Interest Only

This workflow is for public duties, professional history, published work, litigation, sanctions, conflicts of interest, or other clearly legitimate questions. It is not for private-person dossiers.

## Official wanted/fugitive exception

If the target is an **adult currently named in an active, official wanted/fugitive notice**, first verify that status from the issuing authority and then read `wanted-person-location-intelligence.md`.

That guarded workflow may enable bounded public-source location inference for apprehension support, official tip preparation, or verification of a location clue. It does **not** enable private-account access, device or carrier tracking, purchased telemetry, deception, continuous live surveillance, or tactical apprehension guidance.

If official wanted status cannot be verified, stay in this standard people-research workflow and do not infer the person's current location.

## Stages

1. Define the public-interest question and excluded personal data.
2. Resolve identity using name, role, organization, geography, and dates.
3. Prioritize official biographies, filings, professional licenses, court/regulatory records, publications, and direct statements.
4. Use reputable reporting and archives for context.
5. Distinguish verified identity from namesakes.
6. Redact home addresses, private contacts, relatives, and unrelated personal history.

## Do not

- Identify private people by face.
- Track movements or routines outside the verified official wanted-person exception above.
- Aggregate unrelated accounts into a personal dossier.
- Investigate minors or vulnerable individuals.
