# Company and Organization Workflow

## Stages

1. Resolve the exact legal entity, jurisdiction, registration number, and historical names.
2. Collect official registry filings, regulator notices, licenses, sanctions, and court records where applicable.
3. Map declared directors, officers, subsidiaries, brands, and beneficial ownership only from lawful public sources.
4. Review official websites, annual reports, procurement records, patents, and archived pages.
5. Add reputable reporting and industry datasets for context.
6. Build a dated timeline and separate legal entities from brands or operating names.

## Verification traps

- Same-name companies in different jurisdictions.
- Outdated executive or address data.
- Aggregators copying one another.
- Subsidiary, franchise, reseller, and parent relationships being conflated.
