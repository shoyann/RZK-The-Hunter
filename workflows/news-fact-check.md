# News and Fact-Checking Workflow

## Stages

1. Normalize the claim into who/what/where/when and identify the exact disputed element.
2. Find the earliest available source, original statement, document, or media.
3. Compare publication time, event time, timezone, and edit history.
4. Search official records and primary sources.
5. Compare independent reputable reporting, including local-language coverage.
6. Verify images/video separately.
7. Check archives for deleted or changed pages.
8. Identify missing context, satire, old media, miscaptioning, or copied claims.
9. Give a verdict: supported, misleading, unsupported, false, or unresolved—with confidence.
