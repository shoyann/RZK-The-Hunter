# Quick Triage

1. Restate the objective in one sentence, including the exact requested granularity and viewpoint relationship.
2. Identify target type, geography, time range, and known identifiers.
3. Run the safety gate.
4. Create 2–5 research questions and at least two plausible hypotheses where ambiguity exists.
5. For image/video tasks, perform a full-frame clue inventory before searching: scene layers, grid/semantic crops, alternate text readings, clue families, and negative clues.
6. Select 3–7 tools across at least two source classes and, for visual work, across multiple search lanes.
7. Define what would confirm or falsify each question and candidate.
8. Collect only the minimum needed evidence, while preserving source lineage and contradictions.
9. For exact geolocation, verify camera/object/road geometry rather than relying on a nearby address.
10. Report facts, inferences, contradictions, alternatives, and unknowns separately.
