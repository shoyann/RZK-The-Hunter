# Username and Social Workflow

Use only for legitimate research. A shared handle is not identity proof.

## Stages

1. Search the exact username on platform-native search and general search.
2. Use username checkers to generate candidate accounts.
3. Compare public profile signals: creation dates, language, topics, linked domains, avatars, and self-declared identity.
4. Check archives and earliest appearances.
5. Require multiple non-trivial matching signals before linking accounts.
6. Record alternative owners and false-positive risk.

## Stop rules

- Do not access private accounts, social-engineer contacts, or collect unrelated personal details.
- Do not map family, home address, or live location.
- Do not infer sensitive traits from follows, likes, or communities.
