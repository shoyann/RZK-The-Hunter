# Tool Selection Matrix

Choose an evidence-changing action first, then the smallest useful tool set.
Use an available host tool or a known authoritative source directly when it
fits. Consult the catalog only for a capability gap or an explicit tool-selection
request; catalog discovery and availability checks are not routine preflight.

Follow [execution modes](execution-modes.md) for effort and ending outcomes.
Use [adaptive strategy](adaptive-investigation-strategy.md) when choosing among
consequential alternatives. Workflow bundles are suggestions, not required calls
or source quotas.

| Objective | Direct starting point | Add only for a material gap |
|---|---|---|
| Domain / infrastructure | RDAP/WHOIS, DNS or certificate record relevant to the question | ASN/BGP, archives, technology or reputation evidence |
| Company / organization | Official registry, regulator, filing or company statement | Ownership records, archives or independent reporting |
| Username / social | Platform-native public source | Archives or multiple non-trivial identity signals; a username match alone is not identity proof |
| Public-person research | Relevant official biography, filing or public statement | Proportionate public-interest corroboration under the safety policy |
| Email / phone defensive check | Ownership/authorization confirmation and reputable exposure notification | Relevant reputation/validation service; never raw leaks or credentials |
| Image / video | Original media and discriminating visible clues | Exact-object, map geometry, independent mechanism or provenance evidence according to requested precision |
| News / claim verification | Original statement or primary record | Independent reporting, archive or media provenance check |
| Threat intelligence | Official CERT/vendor report or directly relevant IOC record | Defensive enrichment; never sample execution by default |
| Monitoring | Official feed or authorized page-change source | Deduplication, alerts or archive snapshot when required |

## Select proportionately

Prefer direct relevance, authority, suitable time/geographic coverage,
reproducibility, lawful access and independent evidence value. Compare these
qualitatively only when a choice needs resolving. Do not score every candidate
on a fixed numerical rubric or select extra tools to reach a minimum count.

One authoritative source can resolve a direct question. Additional tools must
address an unresolved discriminator or verify a material risk. Different wrappers
around the same map, image index or database do not add source independence.

For precise geolocation, choose evidence mechanisms that establish exact-scene
identity, relevant geometry and the strongest credible near-match. A metadata,
OCR, reverse-image, map and archive tool are available options, not five mandatory
stages. Follow the [visual workflow](../workflows/image-video-geolocation.md).

## Catalog caveat

Catalog descriptions, access requirements and free/paid status may be stale or
promotional. Verify relevant details on the current official site before relying
on an unfamiliar tool. Inclusion is not endorsement or authorization. Do not
refresh the whole catalog during an investigation unless maintenance is part of
the task. People-related and official wanted/fugitive safety gates still apply.
